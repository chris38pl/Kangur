## Order

1. `staging` has #2, #4, #5. Backend deploys there automatically.
2. EAS `preview` builds (iOS and Android) from `release/1.0.8`, TestFlight and Play Closed Testing.
3. Device check (below), then `production` builds and store submission.
4. Promote `staging` to `main` before the store builds reach users: the app calls the new workspace endpoints.
5. After both stores approve: `APP_LATEST_VERSION=1.0.8`.
